Contains information from nmrshiftdb2 (https://nmrshiftdb.nmr.uni-koeln.de/), made available under the nmrshiftdb2 Database License included here.
The two gzip files contain the complete, byte-identical nmrshiftdb.csv entries from the official predictorh.jar/predictorc.jar published 2023-10-03. Source URLs, archive hashes, extracted-content hashes and packaged hashes are in data-manifest.json.
No nmrshiftdb2 executable code is included. The application uses its own streaming lookup and the existing CDK 2.12 HOSECodeGenerator. This is the standard HOSE model, not the upstream ExtendedHOSE stereochemical predictor.
